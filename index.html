How I work

Tools only matter in context. Here is what I actually use each part of the stack for.

List building & enrichment

Clay · Apollo · LinkedIn Sales Navigator · Apify

I build and enrich lists from 5,000 to 20,000+ contacts, using Clay waterfalls and OpenAI columns to qualify and personalize at scale without sacrificing accuracy.

Sending & deliverability

Smartlead · Instantly · EmailBison · Salesfinity

Inbox setup, warmup, domain rotation and volume management. I do not track opens because open tracking hurts deliverability. Reply rate and positive reply rate are the metrics I run on.

Automation & workflows

Make.com · Claude Code · n8n · HeyReach

Connecting the pieces so leads move without manual work: reply detection, enrichment triggers, Slack alerts and multichannel sequencing across email and LinkedIn.

CRM & operations

Pipedrive · ClickUp · Google Sheets

Clean handoffs matter as much as good campaigns. I make sure every positive reply lands in the CRM with full context, and I have run up to six client accounts at the same time.

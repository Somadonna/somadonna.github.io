SaaS campaign: from flat to ~12% positive replies in two weeks

Result
~12% positive reply rate
Stack
Clay · Instantly · Google Sheets
Timeline
Two weeks of angle testing

Context

A SaaS client came in with a campaign that was sending fine but converting poorly. Deliverability was healthy, the list was decent, but replies were flat. The problem was not infrastructure. It was the message.

What I did

Instead of rewriting one email and hoping, I set up structured angle testing. Each variant led with a different problem framing: one focused on cost, one on the manual work the product removed, one on a specific trigger event in the prospect's business. Same list quality, same sending setup, different core angle.

I let each angle run long enough to get a real signal, killed the losers, and doubled down on the framing that prospects actually responded to. To be fair, the winning angle was not the one I expected. That is exactly why I test instead of guessing.

Screenshot placeholder: campaign analytics showing reply breakdown by variant. Add before publishing.

Results

After two weeks of angle testing, the campaign reached around a 12% positive reply rate. Not just replies, but replies expressing genuine interest.

A note on metrics: I do not track or report open rates. Open tracking pixels hurt deliverability, and opens do not tell you anything about intent. Reply rate and positive reply rate are the numbers that reflect whether a campaign is working.

What I would do differently

I would start the angle testing on a smaller sample before scaling volume. We spent some sending capacity on angles that were never going to work. Testing on 10 to 15% of the list first would have gotten to the winner cheaper.

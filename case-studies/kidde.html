KIDDE: the campaign the client asked me to pause

Result
€50,000+ in new pipeline in 2 months
Stack
Clay · Smartlead · Pipedrive
Role
Full campaign ownership

Context

KIDDE is a manufacturing company that needed a steady flow of qualified conversations for their sales team. They had tried outbound before with mixed results, so the bar was not just volume. Leads had to be worth the sales team's time.

What I built

I owned the campaign end to end. That meant building and enriching the target list in Clay, qualifying accounts before a single email went out, setting up the sending infrastructure in Smartlead with proper warmup and domain rotation, and writing copy that spoke to a specific problem rather than pitching a service.

The list work mattered more than anything else. Instead of blasting a broad list, I used Clay enrichment and qualification columns to cut the list down to companies that actually matched the offer. Smaller list, better replies.

Results

The campaign generated over €50,000 in new pipeline in two months. It got to the point where the client asked me to pause it because their team could not keep up with the volume of leads coming in.

Getting asked to slow down is an unusual problem to have in outbound, and honestly it is the best kind of feedback a campaign can get. It also exposed a real lesson: pipeline generation and pipeline handling are two different capacities, and a campaign should be paced to match what the sales team can absorb.

What I would do differently

I would agree on a lead handling capacity with the client before launch and throttle sending volume to match it. Pausing a campaign that is working costs momentum. Pacing it from day one keeps deliverability, reply handling and sales capacity in sync.

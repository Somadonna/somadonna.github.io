The Clay workflow that cut lead handoff from hours to minutes

Result
Handoff time: hours → minutes
Stack
Clay · Smartlead · Slack · Pipedrive
Type
Automation build

The problem

Positive replies were sitting unanswered for hours. Someone had to notice the reply in the sending tool, look up the lead, copy their details into the CRM, and ping whoever was booking calls. Every step was manual, and speed to lead matters. A prospect who replied at 9am and heard back at 2pm is a colder prospect.

What I built

An automated pipeline with reply detection at the front. When a reply came in, the workflow classified it, and positive replies triggered the rest of the chain automatically. The core of the build is what happens next: the workflow runs a phone number waterfall in Clay for every interested lead, so by the time a human sees the reply, they already have a number to call.

I recorded a full walkthrough of the workflow, including the phone number enrichment:

The Slack message was not just a notification. It carried the enriched context: who the person is, company details, their phone number, what campaign and angle they replied to, and the reply itself. Whoever picked it up could respond immediately without opening three other tools, or just pick up the phone. In parallel, the same data created a deal in Pipedrive so nothing depended on someone remembering to log it.

Results

Handoff time went from hours to minutes. Positive replies got answered while the prospect was still at their desk, with a phone number already attached, and the CRM stayed clean without anyone doing data entry.

Why this matters beyond one client

Most outbound problems past a certain scale are operations problems, not copy problems. Campaigns that generate replies are wasted if the replies leak out of a manual process. This kind of connective work between tools is, honestly, where I think a GTM engineer earns the title.
